Water content, off the same diagram \(w=\dfrac{W_w}{W_s}=\) \(\dfrac{Se\,\gw}{\Gs\gw}\) \(=\dfrac{Se}{\Gs}\),  so  \(Se=w\Gs\) \(S\) and \(w\) both measure water — one by volume, one by weight. \(e\) and \(\Gs\) convert between them.
The same \(\gamma_t\), written in terms of \(w\) Substituting \(Se=w\Gs\) into step 5 gives the form to use when \(w\) is measured and \(S\) is not:  \(\gamma_t=\) \(\dfrac{\Gs(1+w)\,\gw}{1+e}\)
