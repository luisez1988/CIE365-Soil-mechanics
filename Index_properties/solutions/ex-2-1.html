Start with definition of porosity \(n=\) \(\cfrac{V_v}{V_t}\)
Invert the equation \(\cfrac{1}{n}=\) \(\cfrac{V_t}{V_v}\)
Decompose total volume: \(\cfrac{1}{n}=\) \(\cfrac{V_s+V_v}{V_v}=\) \(\cfrac{V_s}{V_v} + 1\)
Use the definition of void ratio \(\cfrac{1}{n}=\) \(\cfrac{1}{e} + 1=\) \(\cfrac{1+e}{e}\)
take the reciprocal on both sides \(n=\) \(\cfrac{e}{1+e}\) \(\implies \ e= \) \(\cfrac{n}{1-n}\)
