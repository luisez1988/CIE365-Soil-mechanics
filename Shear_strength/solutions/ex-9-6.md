## Statement

> Point $A$ is at the middle of the base of the shaded slice ($\alpha=40^\circ$). The element shows the total stresses at $A$, and the water table, which seeps out on the slope face, is $h_w=4$ m above $A$. Soil: $\phi'_p=36^\circ$, $c'_p=10$ kPa; $\phi'_c=32^\circ$, $c'_c=0$. Determine: (a) the factor of safety on the slice base, (b) the effective stress Mohr's circle at $A$ against the M-C lines, and (c) the principal stresses.

The figure is the slope of the applications slide with the generic element removed. The water table now falls to the left and exits on the slope face, with a seepage face down to the toe. One slice is shaded, $A$ is at the middle of its base, and $h_w$ and $\alpha$ are marked. The inset element shows the total stresses at $A$: $170\ \mathrm{kPa}$ vertical, $90\ \mathrm{kPa}$ horizontal, and $15\ \mathrm{kPa}$ shear drawn as in Example 9.1 (top face to the left, right face down).

## Given / Find

- $\sigma_y = 170\ \mathrm{kPa}$, $\sigma_x = 90\ \mathrm{kPa}$, $\tau_{xy} = -15\ \mathrm{kPa}$ (clockwise), $\alpha = 40^\circ$, $h_w = 4\ \mathrm{m}$, $\gamma_w = 9.81\ \mathrm{kN/m^3}$
- Peak: $\phi'_p = 36^\circ$, $c'_p = 10\ \mathrm{kPa}$. Critical: $\phi'_c = 32^\circ$, $c'_c = 0$
- **Find:** (a) $FS$ on the base; (b) effective Mohr's circle against the M-C lines; (c) $\sigma_1$, $\sigma_3$

## Assumptions

The sign convention is the one of the State of stress deck and Example 9.1: the shear drawn on the element is clockwise, so $\tau_{xy} = -15\ \mathrm{kPa}$, and $\alpha$ is measured counterclockwise from the horizontal, with $\sigma_\alpha = \dfrac{\sigma_x+\sigma_y}{2} + \dfrac{\sigma_y-\sigma_x}{2}\cos 2\alpha + \tau_{xy}\sin 2\alpha$ and $\tau_\alpha = \dfrac{\sigma_y-\sigma_x}{2}\sin 2\alpha - \tau_{xy}\cos 2\alpha$. The base rises to the right, so $\alpha = +40^\circ$. With this choice the shear on the element pushes the soil down the slope, toward the toe.

The pore pressure at $A$ is $u = \gamma_w h_w$, with $h_w$ the vertical height of the water table above $A$. With seepage toward the face the equipotentials are curved, so this slightly overestimates $u$. It is the usual hand-calculation approximation.

The factor of safety is defined on the base of the slice as $FS = \tau_f/\tau_\alpha$, the definition on the applications slide.

## Solution

Effective stress controls strength, so the pore pressure is subtracted from the normal stresses. Shear stresses are not changed by $u$.

1. Pore pressure at $A$ — $u = \gamma_w h_w = 9.81 \times 4 = 39.2\ \mathrm{kPa}$
2. Total stresses on the base — $\sigma_\alpha = \dfrac{90+170}{2} + \dfrac{170-90}{2}\cos 80^\circ + (-15)\sin 80^\circ = 130 + 6.95 - 14.77 = 122.2\ \mathrm{kPa}$ and $\tau_\alpha = \dfrac{170-90}{2}\sin 80^\circ - (-15)\cos 80^\circ = 39.39 + 2.60 = 42.0\ \mathrm{kPa}$
3. Effective normal stress on the base — $\sigma'_\alpha = \sigma_\alpha - u = 122.2 - 39.2 = 82.9\ \mathrm{kPa}$
4. (a) Peak strength — $\tau_f = c'_p + \sigma'_\alpha \tan\phi'_p = 10 + 82.9 \tan 36^\circ = 70.2\ \mathrm{kPa}$, so $FS_p = \dfrac{70.2}{42.0} = \mathbf{1.67}$
5. (a) Critical strength — $\tau_f = \sigma'_\alpha \tan\phi'_c = 82.9 \tan 32^\circ = 51.8\ \mathrm{kPa}$, so $FS_c = \dfrac{51.8}{42.0} = \mathbf{1.23}$
6. (b) Effective stress Mohr's circle — $\sigma'_x = 90 - 39.2 = 50.8$, $\sigma'_y = 170 - 39.2 = 130.8\ \mathrm{kPa}$, $\tau_{xy} = -15\ \mathrm{kPa}$. Centre $\dfrac{\sigma'_x+\sigma'_y}{2} = 90.8\ \mathrm{kPa}$, radius $\sqrt{\left(\dfrac{\sigma'_y-\sigma'_x}{2}\right)^2 + \tau_{xy}^2} = \sqrt{40^2+15^2} = 42.7\ \mathrm{kPa}$
7. (b) Comparison with the M-C lines — the distance from the centre to the critical line is $90.8 \sin 32^\circ = 48.1\ \mathrm{kPa}$, and to the peak line $\left(90.8 + 10\cot 36^\circ\right)\sin 36^\circ = 61.4\ \mathrm{kPa}$. Both exceed the radius $42.7\ \mathrm{kPa}$, so **the circle lies below both lines**: the largest mobilized angle is $\arcsin(42.7/90.8) = 28.1^\circ < 32^\circ$. The base itself mobilizes $\arctan(42.0/82.9) = 26.9^\circ$, so it is not the most stressed plane through $A$.
8. (c) Principal stresses — $\sigma'_{1,3} = 90.8 \pm 42.7$: $\mathbf{\sigma'_1 = 133.5\ kPa}$ and $\mathbf{\sigma'_3 = 48.0\ kPa}$; in total stress $\mathbf{\sigma_1 = 172.7\ kPa}$ and $\mathbf{\sigma_3 = 87.3\ kPa}$. $\sigma_1$ acts on the plane at $\alpha = \tfrac{1}{2}\arctan\dfrac{2(-15)}{170-90} = -10.3^\circ$, and $\sigma_3$ on the plane at $79.7^\circ$.

The normal stress at $\alpha = -10.3^\circ$ equals $\sigma_1$, which confirms the principal direction. The sign of $\tau_{xy}$ was checked with a traction vector on the $40^\circ$ plane: a leftward shear on the top face gives the deck's $\tau_{xy} < 0$ and a down-slope shear on the base, as expected in a slope facing left.
