Pore pressure at \(A\) \(u=\gamma_w h_w=\) \(9.81\times4\) \(=\) 39.2 \(\unit{kPa}\) \(h_w\) is measured vertically up to the water table

Normal stress on the base
\(\sigma_n=\dfrac{\sigma_x+\sigma_y}{2}+\dfrac{\sigma_y-\sigma_x}{2}\cos2 \alpha+\tau_{xy}\sin2 \alpha\)
\(\sigma_n=\) \(130+40\cos80^\circ-15\sin80^\circ\) \(=\) 122.2 \(\unit{kPa}\) Clockwise shear: \(\tau_{xy}=-15\unit{kPa}\), as in Example 9.1
Shear stress on the base
\(\tau_m=\dfrac{\sigma_y-\sigma_x}{2}\sin2 \alpha-\tau_{xy}\cos2 \alpha\)
\(\tau_m=\) \(40\sin80^\circ+15\cos80^\circ\) \(=\) 42.0 \(\unit{kPa}\)

Effective normal stress
\(\sigma'_n=\sigma_n-u=\) \(122.2-39.2\) \(=\) 82.9 \(\unit{kPa}\) Water can't carry shear stress, only normal stress changes

(a) Peak strength
\(\tau_f=c'_p+\sigma'_n\tan\phi'_p=\) \(10+82.9\tan36^\circ\) \(=\) 70.2 \(\unit{kPa}\)
\(\tau_m=\) \(42.0\) \(\unit{kPa}\)
\(FS_p=\dfrac{\tau_f}{\tau_m}=\) \(\dfrac{70.2}{42.0}\) \(=\) 1.67 \(m\) stands for mobilized shear stress at the base

(a) Critical strength
\(\tau_f=\sigma'_n\tan\phi'_c=\) \(82.9\tan32^\circ\) \(=\) 51.8 \(\unit{kPa}\)
\(\tau_m=\) \(42.0\) \(\unit{kPa}\)
\(FS_c=\dfrac{\tau_f}{\tau_m}=\) \(\dfrac{51.8}{42.0}\) \(=\) 1.23 Use \(\phi'_c\) with \(c'=0\) for large deformations
(b) Effective stresses at \(A\)
\(\sigma'_x=\) \(90-39.2\) \(=\) 50.8 \(\unit{kPa}\)
\(\sigma'_y=\) \(170-39.2\) \(=\) 130.8 \(\unit{kPa}\)

(b) Circle
\(\sigma_m=\dfrac{\sigma'_x+\sigma'_y}{2}=\) 90.8 \(\unit{kPa}\)
radius \(=\sqrt{\left(\dfrac{\sigma'_y-\sigma'_x}{2}\right)^2+\tau_{xy}^2}=\) 42.7 \(\unit{kPa}\)

(c) Effective principal stresses
\(\sigma'_{1,3}=\text{centre}\pm\text{radius}=\) \(90.8\pm42.7\)
\(\sigma'_1=\) 133.5 \(\unit{kPa}\)   \(\sigma'_3=\) 48.0 \(\unit{kPa}\)
(c) Total principal stresses
\(\sigma=\sigma'+u\):   \(\sigma_1=\) 172.7 \(\unit{kPa}\)   \(\sigma_3=\) 87.3 \(\unit{kPa}\)
(c) Orientation
\(n=\dfrac{1}{2}\arctan\left(\dfrac{2\tau_{xy}}{\sigma_y-\sigma_x}\right)=\) \(\dfrac{1}{2}\arctan\left(\dfrac{2(-15)}{170-90}\right)\) \(=\) −10.3\(^\circ\) \(\sigma_1\) acts on this plane; \(\sigma_3\) on the plane at 79.7°
