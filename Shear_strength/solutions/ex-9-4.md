## Statement

> The figure shows the results of three direct shear tests on a silty sand sample. Find: (a) the peak friction angle, and (b) the secant friction angle at $\delta x/D=0.05$. $D=2.5$ in.

The figure is `FiguresGeneral/DST_data.svg`: shear stress (psi) and vertical displacement (mil) against horizontal displacement (in) for normal stresses of $10.4$, $15$ and $20.6\ \mathrm{psi}$. Its click-drawn guide lines mark the peaks (grey) and the values at $\delta x = 0.125\ \mathrm{in}$ (green).

## Given / Find

- $\sigma'_n = 10.4,\ 15,\ 20.6\ \mathrm{psi}$; $D = 2.5\ \mathrm{in}$; $\tau$ against $\delta x$ from the figure
- **Find:** (a) $\phi'_p$; (b) $\phi'_s$ at $\delta x/D = 0.05$

## Assumptions

The test is drained, so the applied normal stresses are effective stresses. This is the author's reading on EX740, where the table is headed $\sigma'_n$.

The silty sand has no cohesion, so each envelope is drawn through the origin, as on EX741. A free least-squares line gives a small intercept ($1.2\ \mathrm{psi}$ for the peak), which is within the reading error of the figure; the Flags section records it.

## Solution

The Mohr-Coulomb line $\tau_f = c' + \sigma'_n \tan\phi'$ is plotted through the three $(\sigma'_n, \tau)$ points of each condition; with $c' = 0$ its slope is $\tan\phi'$.

1. Data — read from the curves (author's table on EX740):

| $\sigma'_n$ (psi) | $\tau_p$ (psi) | $\tau_s$ (psi) |
|---|---|---|
| 20.6 | 19.1 | 18.4 |
| 15 | 14.4 | 13.4 |
| 10.4 | 10.2 | 9.5 |

2. Principles — $\tau_f = c' + \sigma'_n \tan\phi'$ (author's line on EX740)
3. (a) Peak envelope — plot the three peak points in $\tau$ against $\sigma'_n$ and draw the line through the origin (author's green line on EX741): $\tan\phi'_p = \dfrac{\sum \sigma'_n \tau_p}{\sum \sigma'^2_n} = 0.945$, so $\mathbf{\phi'_p \approx 43^\circ}$. One point alone gives the same answer: $\arctan(19.1/20.6) = 42.8^\circ$.
4. (b) Horizontal displacement for the secant condition — $\delta x = 0.05 D = 0.05 \times 2.5 = 0.125\ \mathrm{in}$ (author's line on EX740)
5. (b) Shear stresses at $\delta x = 0.125\ \mathrm{in}$ — read on each curve: $18.4$, $13.4$ and $9.5\ \mathrm{psi}$ (third column of the table)
6. (b) Secant envelope — line through the origin and the three secant points (author's red line on EX741): $\tan\phi'_s = 0.896$, so $\mathbf{\phi'_s \approx 42^\circ}$
