State of stress

Geotechnical engineers often require to analyze stresses angles. E.g: to calculate the bearing capacity of a foundation.

Stress is a measure of internal forces acting on a continuum body. At any point in the body, a of stresses is acting. We call this a .

Stress in 2D

In some situations, the stress state can be represented in 2D. In this case, the stress tensor is reduced to a . The following are the most common stress states in 2D:

Plane stress

Strain in one direction is zero

\( \begin{align} \boldsymbol{\sigma} = \begin{bmatrix} \sigma_{xx} & \tau_{xy} & 0\\ \tau_{xy} & \sigma_{yy} & 0\\ 0 & 0 & \sigma_{zz}\\ \end{bmatrix} \end{align} \)

Axysimmetric stress

Symmetric around the z-axis

\( \begin{align} \boldsymbol{\sigma} = \begin{bmatrix} \sigma_{r} & 0 & \tau_{rz}\\ 0 & \sigma_{\theta} & 0\\ \tau_{zr} & 0 & \sigma_{zz}\\ \end{bmatrix} \end{align} \)
