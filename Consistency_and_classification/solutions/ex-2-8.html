This soil does not fit on the printed chart. The figure stops at \(LL=110\) and \(PI=60\), so there is nowhere to plot \(LL=124\), \(PI=77\) and the A-line equation is the only route. Take care with it: dropping the \(-20\) and writing \(0.73\times124\) lands the point below the A-line and returns the wrong symbol, with nothing in the arithmetic to warn you. The U-line is the companion check — no real soil plots above it, so a point that does means the test, not the soil, is wrong.
