Identify system Water crosses three materials in a row: stone, soil, stone \(\implies\) flow in series
\(\boxed{\dfrac{L_{tot}}{k_{eq}}=\displaystyle\sum_i\dfrac{L_i}{k_i}}\).
Simplify to "resistance" terms Define \(R=L/k \ \implies\) \(R_{tot}=\) \(2R_{stone}+R_{soil}\)
Geometry, in consistent units \(D=6''=\) 15.24 cm,   \(L_{tot}=12''=\) 30.48 cm,   \(t_{stone}=\tfrac12''=\) 1.27 cm
\(L_{soil}=L_{tot}-2\,t_{stone}=\) \(30.48-2(1.27)\) \(=\) 27.94 cm
Cross-sectional area \(A=\dfrac{\pi D^{2}}{4}=\) \(\dfrac{\pi(15.24)^{2}}{4}\) \(=\) 182.4 \(\unit{cm^2}\)
Calculate \(k_{eq}\) The test measures the permenability of the whole system, so it returns \(k_{eq}\):
\(k_{eq}=\dfrac{V\,L_{tot}}{A\,\Delta h\,t}=\) \(\dfrac{\textcolor{red}{\overbrace{\textcolor{navy}{2000}}^{Q \text{lt} \rightarrow \text{cm}^3}}\times30.48}{182.4\times \textcolor{red}{\underbrace{\textcolor{navy}{100}}_{\Delta h \ \text{m to cm}}} \times \textcolor{red}{\underbrace{\textcolor{navy}{5400}}_{t \ \text{hr to sec}}}}\) \(=\) \(6.19\times10^{-4}\) \(\unit{cm/s}\)
Total resistance measured \(R_{tot}=\dfrac{L_{tot}}{k_{eq}}=\) \(\dfrac{30.48}{6.19\times10^{-4}}\) \(=\) \(49,250\) \(\unit{s}\)
Resistance the stones are responsible for \(2R_{stone}=\dfrac{2\,t_{stone}}{k_{stone}}=\) \(\dfrac{2 \times1.27}{1\times10^{-3}}\) \(=\) \(2,540\) \(\unit{s}\)
Permeability of the sand \(k_{soil}=\dfrac{L_{soil}}{R_{tot}-2R_{stone}}=\) \(\dfrac{27.94}{49\,250-2\,540}\) \(=\) \(5.98\times10^{-4}\) \(\unit{cm/s}\)
Worth the effort? The stones carry 5.2% of the total resistance. Ignoring them overestimates the sand's permeability by 3.5%. Error grows quickly as the soil becomes more permeable relative to the stones, which is when you are most tempted to skip the correction
