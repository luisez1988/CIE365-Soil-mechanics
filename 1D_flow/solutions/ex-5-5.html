Well levels interpretation The water level in an open well is total head \(\implies\) \(h_A=18.4\unit{m}\) and \(h_B=17.1\unit{m}\)
Hydraulic gradient \(i=\dfrac{h_A-h_B}{L}=\) \(\dfrac{18.4-17.1}{20}\) \(=\) 0.065
Average linear velocity \(\vs=\dfrac{L}{\Delta t}=\) \(\dfrac{20}{167} \times \underbrace{\dfrac{100}{3600}}_{\text{Convert to cm/s}}\) \(=\) \(\ee{3.33}{-3}\) \(\unit{cm/s}\)
Darcy velocity Darcy's velocity is measure in the bulk, not the pores, so it is smaller than \(\vs\):
\(\vD=n_e\,\vs=\) \(0.25\times\ee{3.33}{-3}\) \(=\) \(\ee{8.32}{-4}\) \(\unit{cm/s}\)
Hydraulic conductivity From \(\vD=k\,i\):   \(k=\dfrac{\vD}{i}=\) \(\dfrac{\ee{8.32}{-4}}{0.065}\) \(=\) \(\ee{1.28}{-2}\) \(\unit{cm/s}\) about 11 m/day
