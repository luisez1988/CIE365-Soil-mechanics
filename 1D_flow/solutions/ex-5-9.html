Identify system The water crosses the three sands side by side \(implies\) Layers in parallel:
\(\keq=\dfrac{\sum k_iA_i}{A}\)
Head difference \(\Delta h=\) \(137.23-125.5\) \(=\) 11.73 \(\unit{ft}\)
Flow path length The layers drop \(13\unit{ft}\) over \(1255.6\unit{ft} \implies\) \(L=\) \(\sqrt{1255.6^{2}+13^{2}}=\) 1255.7 \(\unit{ft}\)
Hydraulic gradient \(i=\dfrac{\Delta h}{L}=\) \(\dfrac{11.73}{1255.7}\) \(=\) \(\ee{9.34}{-3}\)
Equivalent permeability Unit width, so areas go as thicknesses: \(\keq=\dfrac{\sum k_ib_i}{\sum b_i}=\) \(\dfrac{\textcolor{red}{\overbrace{\textcolor{navy}{(\ee{1.5}{-5})(3)}}^{\text{SC layer}}}+\textcolor{red}{\overbrace{\textcolor{navy}{(\ee{7.89}{-3})(3)}}^{\text{SP layer}}}+\textcolor{red}{\overbrace{\textcolor{navy}{(\ee{1.2}{-4})(4)}}^{\text{SM layer}}}}{10}\) \(=\) \(\ee{2.42}{-3}\) \(\unit{cm/s}\)
Seepage per unit length of channel
\(q=\keq\,i\,A=\) \((\ee{2.42}{-3})(\ee{9.34}{-3}) \textcolor{red}{\underbrace{\textcolor{navy}{(304.8)}}_{\text{ft to cm}}} \textcolor{red}{\underbrace{\textcolor{navy}{\times 1}}_{\substack{\text{implicit}\\ \text{unit length}}}} \) \(=\) \(\ee{6.89}{-3}\) \(\unit{cm^3/s}\) per cm
Transform units Once done, transform to \(\unit{Lt/day}\) per \(\unit{ft}\) of channel:
\(q=\ee{6.89}{-3}\dfrac{\unit{cm^3}}{\unit{s}\cdot\unit{cm}}\times\) \(\dfrac{1\unit{Lt}}{1000\unit{cm^3}}\) \(\times\) \(\dfrac{30.48\unit{cm}}{1\unit{ft}}\) \(\times\) \(\dfrac{86\,400\unit{s}}{1\unit{day}}\) \(=\) 18.1 \(\unit{Lt/day/ft}\) of channel
