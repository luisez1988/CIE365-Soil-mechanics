Start at the boundaries The standpipes at the ends read their own reservoirs, hence:
\(h_A=280\unit{mm}=\) 28 \(\unit{cm}\)
\(h_D=200\unit{mm}=\) 20 \(\unit{cm}\)
Hydraulic gradient \(L_{AD}=3\times60=\) 18 \(\unit{cm}\), so \(i=\dfrac{h_A-h_D}{L_{AD}}=\) \(\dfrac{28-20}{18}\) \(=\) 0.444
Heads and pressure at the interior points The loss of head per interval is \(\Delta h= i\times\Delta L=\) \(0.444 \times 6\) \(=\) 2.67 \(\unit{cm}\)
