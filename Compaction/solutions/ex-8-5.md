## Statement

> The construction of an earth embankment is designed in lifts of 6 inches with the same soil studied in Example 8.4. What are the minimum dry density and the range of water contents that the soil could be compacted with in the field if the relative compaction specified is: (i) 98%, (ii) 95%?

## Given / Find

- From Example 8.4: $\gamma_{d,max} = 106\ \mathrm{pcf}$, $w_{opt} = 17.6\%$, $G_s = 2.67$, and the compaction curve
- $RC = 98\%$ and $RC = 95\%$
- **Find:** the minimum $\gamma_{d,field}$ and the range of $w$ for each $RC$

## Assumptions

The water content limits are read where the horizontal line for each $\gamma_{d,field}$ crosses the compaction curve of Example 8.4, as in the handwritten solution.

The dry limit for $RC = 95\%$ comes from the test point at $w = 10\%$, $\gamma_d = 99.5\ \mathrm{pcf}$, added to Example 8.4 on review. Without that point the data does not reach $100.7\ \mathrm{pcf}$ on the dry side, and the handwritten table has "?" in this cell.

The lift thickness of 6 inches does not enter the calculation.

## Solution

Relative compaction compares the field dry unit weight with the Proctor maximum, $RC = \dfrac{\gamma_{d,field}}{\gamma_{d,max}} \times 100$. Solving for $\gamma_{d,field}$ gives the minimum dry unit weight the contractor has to reach.

1. Sample calculation, $RC = 98\%$ — $\gamma_{d,field} = 0.98 \times \gamma_{d,max} = 0.98 \times 106 = \mathbf{103.9\ \mathrm{pcf}}$
2. $RC = 95\%$ — $\gamma_{d,field} = 0.95 \times 106 = \mathbf{100.7\ \mathrm{pcf}}$
3. Read the water contents off the curve, shown in the table.

| $RC$ | Min $\gamma_{d,field}$ (pcf) | $w$ range (%) |
|---|---|---|
| $98\%$ | $\mathbf{103.9}$ | $\mathbf{13.9}$ to $\mathbf{21.6}$ |
| $95\%$ | $\mathbf{100.7}$ | $\mathbf{11.0}$ to $\mathbf{24.2}$ |


With the point at $w = 10\%$ added to the data, from the natural cubic spline of Example 8.4:

```
98% [13.87, 21.59] 95% [10.98, 24.22]
```

The dry unit weights were recomputed in Python. The water contents were read from the path data of the green curve in `EX350.svg`, not by eye, so they are only as good as that curve. The guide lines drawn in the figure sit at $13.85\%$, $21.59\%$ and $24.17\%$, which agrees. Units: $RC$ is a ratio, so $\gamma_{d,field}$ carries the units of $\gamma_{d,max}$. The range narrows as $RC$ rises and it brackets $w_{opt}$, as it should.
