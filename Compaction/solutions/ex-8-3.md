## Statement

> Find the maximum dry density of a soil compacted with 6% of water content and $G_s=2.66$. Plot the ZAV and lines of equal saturation at 80% and 60%.

## Given / Find

- $w = 6\%$, $G_s = 2.66$
- **Find:** $\gamma_{d,max}$ and the plot of the ZAV with the lines $S = 80\%$ and $S = 60\%$

## Assumptions

"Maximum dry density" means the theoretical value at $S = 100\%$ from the ZAV equation on the previous slide. The handwritten solution reads it this way and reports a dry unit weight.

The result is given in pcf with $\gamma_w = 62.4\ \mathrm{pcf}$ and in $\mathrm{kN/m^3}$ with $\gamma_w = 9.81\ \mathrm{kN/m^3}$, because the handwritten solution shows both.

The handwritten solution contains no plot. On review, a table of water contents and the plot were added as a second slide, replacing the Google Sheet slide.

## Solution

$\gamma_d$ is largest when all the air is removed, so the theoretical limit is the ZAV equation, $\gamma_{d,max} = \dfrac{\gamma_w G_s}{1 + w G_s}$.

1. Unit weight of water — $\gamma_w = 9.81\ \mathrm{kN/m^3}$ or $\gamma_w = 62.4\ \mathrm{pcf}$
2. In pcf — $\gamma_{d,max} = \dfrac{62.4 \times 2.66}{1 + 0.06 \times 2.66} = \mathbf{143.1\ \mathrm{pcf}}$
3. In $\mathrm{kN/m^3}$ — $\gamma_{d,max} = \dfrac{9.81 \times 2.66}{1 + 0.06 \times 2.66} = \mathbf{22.5\ \mathrm{kN/m^3}}$

4. Repeat for a list of water contents — the lines of equal saturation come from $\gamma_d = \dfrac{\gamma_w G_s}{1 + w G_s/S}$, the equation on the "Theoretical maximum dry density" slide. The table is printed in full beside the plot `FiguresGeneral/ex-8-3-zav.svg`.
