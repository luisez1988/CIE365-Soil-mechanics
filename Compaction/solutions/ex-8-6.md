## Statement

> A balloon densimeter test was conducted in the embankment of Example 8.5. The reading on the densimeter indicates that the carved volume is 0.03 ft$^3$. The extracted sample weighs 3.8 lb wet and 3.1 lb dry. Calculate the following:
>
> 1. The in-situ dry unit weight, water content, saturation, and void ratio
> 2. Would this site pass a QC inspection if at least $RC[\%]\geq 98\%$ ?

## Given / Find

- $V_t = 0.03\ \mathrm{ft^3}$, $W_t = 3.8\ \mathrm{lb}$, $W_s = 3.1\ \mathrm{lb}$
- From Example 8.4: $G_s = 2.67$, $\gamma_{d,max} = 106\ \mathrm{pcf}$
- **Find:** $\gamma_{d,field}$, $w$, $S$, $e$, and whether $RC \geq 98\%$

## Assumptions

None — the statement is complete. $G_s$ and $\gamma_{d,max}$ carry over because the embankment uses the soil of Example 8.4. $\gamma_w = 62.4\ \mathrm{pcf}$.

## Solution

The principle is phase relationships: the weights and the volume give $\gamma_d$ and $w$, then $\gamma_d = \dfrac{\gamma_w G_s}{1 + e}$ gives $e$, and $Se = wG_s$ gives $S$.

1. Dry unit weight — $\gamma_{d,field} = \dfrac{W_s}{V_t} = \dfrac{3.1}{0.03} = \mathbf{103.3\ \mathrm{pcf}}$
2. Water content — $w = \dfrac{W_w}{W_s} \times 100 = \dfrac{W_t - W_s}{W_s} \times 100 = \dfrac{3.8 - 3.1}{3.1} \times 100 = \mathbf{22.6\%}$
3. Void ratio — $\gamma_d = \dfrac{\gamma_w G_s}{1 + e} \Rightarrow 1 + e = \dfrac{\gamma_w}{\gamma_d}G_s = \dfrac{62.4}{103.33} \times 2.67 = 1.612$, so $e = \mathbf{0.612}$
4. Saturation — $Se = wG_s \Rightarrow S = \dfrac{wG_s}{e} = \dfrac{0.2258 \times 2.67}{0.612} = \mathbf{98.5\%}$
5. Relative compaction — $RC[\%] = \dfrac{\gamma_{d,field}}{\gamma_{d,max}} \times 100 = \dfrac{103.3}{106} \times 100 = \mathbf{97.5\%}$. Since $97.5\% < 98\%$, **the site does not pass QC.**
