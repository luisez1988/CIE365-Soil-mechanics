Data \(n_{max}=0.476\), \(n_{min}=0.26\), \(e=0.5\), \(G_s=2.65\)
Principles
\(D_r=\dfrac{e_{max}-\overbrace{e}^{\textcolor{red}{\substack{\text{current} \\ \text{void} \\ \text{ratio}}}}}{e_{max}-e_{min}}\times100\)   \(\underbrace{\gamma_d}_{\textcolor{red}{\text{dry density}}}=\dfrac{\gamma_w G_s}{1+e}\)
Calculate \(e_{max}\) and \(e_{min}\)
Recall: \(n=\dfrac{e}{1+e}\Rightarrow e=\dfrac{n}{1-n}\)
\(e_{max}=\dfrac{n_{max}}{1-n_{max}}=\) \(\dfrac{0.476}{0.524}\) \(=\) 0.908
\(e_{min}=\dfrac{n_{min}}{1-n_{min}}=\) \(\dfrac{0.26}{0.74}\) \(=\) 0.351
Calculate relative density
\(D_r=\) \(\dfrac{0.908-\overbrace{0.5}^{\textcolor{red}{\substack{\text{current} \\ \text{void} \\ \text{ratio}}}}}{0.908-0.351}\times100\) \(=\) 73.3 \(\%\)
Calculate max. and min. dry density
\(\gamma_{d,max}=\dfrac{\gamma_w G_s}{1+e_{min}}=\) \(\dfrac{\overbrace{62.4}^{\textcolor{red}{\text{lb/ft}^3}}\times2.65}{1+0.351}\) \(=\) 122 \(\unit{pcf}\)
\(\gamma_{d,min}=\dfrac{\gamma_w G_s}{1+e_{max}}=\) \(\dfrac{62.4\times2.65}{1+0.908}\) \(=\) 86.7 \(\unit{pcf}\)
