Data \(h_d=45.7\unit{cm}\), \(m_h=4.54\unit{kg}\)
\(V=943\unit{cm^3}\), \(N_b=25\)/layer, \(N_l=5\)
Principles \(E_{comp}=m_h g\,\dfrac{h_d N_b N_l}{V}\)
Note: this equation is unit-consistent
Substitute in SI units
\(E_{comp}=\) \(\underbrace{4.54\times9.81}_{\textcolor{red}{\text{N}}}\times\underbrace{\dfrac{45.7\times25\times5}{943}}_{\textcolor{red}{\text{cm/cm}^3}}\) \(=\) 270 \(\unit{N\,cm/cm^3}\)
Convert to kJ/m\(^3\)
\(E_{comp}=270\unit{N\,cm/cm^3}\times\dfrac{(100\unit{cm})^2}{\unit{m^2}}=\) 2,700,000 \(\overbrace{\unit{N\,m}}^{\textcolor{red}{\text{Jules}}}/\unit{m^3}=\) 2,700 \(\unit{kJ/m^3}\)
Convert the data to US units
\(h_d=45.7\unit{cm}=\) 18 \(\unit{in}\)   \(m_h=4.54\unit{kg}=\) 10 \(\unit{lb}\)   \(V=\) 1/30 \(\unit{ft^3}\)
Substitute in US units
\(E_{comp}=\) \(\underbrace{10}_{\textcolor{red}{\substack{\text{no gravity since}\\\text{lb is force}}}}\times\dfrac{\overbrace{(18/12)}^{\textcolor{red}{\text{in ft}}}\times25\times5}{1/30}\) \(=\) 56,250 \(\unit{lb\text{-}ft/ft^3}\)
