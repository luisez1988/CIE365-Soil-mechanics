Data Sand: \(e=0.76\), \(S=100\%\), \(G_s=2.65\)
Clay: \(w=43\%\), \(S=100\%\), \(C_c=0.3\), \(G_s=2.7\), \(\Delta\sigma_v=200\unit{kPa}\)
Principles \(\delta_{pc}=C_c\dfrac{H_0}{1+e_0}\log\dfrac{\sigma'_{vf}}{\sigma'_{vo}}\) No \(OCR\) is given, so the clay is normally consolidated: virgin curve.
Let's find the unit weights first
\(\gamma_{sat,sand}=\dfrac{\gamma_w(G_s+e)}{1+e}=\) \(\dfrac{9.81(2.65+0.76)}{1.76}\) \(=\) 19.01 \(\unit{kN/m^3}\) The statement says the sand above the water table is saturated too.
For the clay \(e_0=wG_s=\) \(0.43\times2.7\) \(=\) 1.161
\(\gamma_{sat,clay}=\dfrac{\gamma_w(G_s+e_0)}{1+e_0}=\) \(\dfrac{9.81(2.7+1.161)}{2.161}\) \(=\) 17.53 \(\unit{kN/m^3}\) \(e_0=wG_s\) holds because the clay is saturated (\(S=1\)).
Now we can calculate \(\sigma'_{vo}\) at mid point of layer
\(\sigma'_{vo}=\overbrace{\underbrace{\gamma_{sand}\,z_{sand}}_{\textcolor{red}{\text{sand}}}+\underbrace{\gamma_{clay}\,z_{clay}}_{\textcolor{red}{\text{clay}}}}^{\textcolor{red}{\text{total stress}}}-\underbrace{\gamma_w\,z_w}_{\textcolor{red}{\text{pore pressure}}}=\) \(9\times19.01+5\times17.53-9.81\times11\) \(=\) 150.8 \(\unit{kPa}\) Pore pressure is measured from the water table, not from the ground surface.
Calculate final effective stress \(\sigma'_{vf}=\sigma'_{vo}+\Delta\sigma_v=\) \(150.8+200\) \(=\) 350.8 \(\unit{kPa}\)
Calculate p.c. settlement
\(\delta_{pc}=\) \(\dfrac{0.3\times10}{2.161}\log\dfrac{350.8}{150.8}\) \(=\) 0.509 \(\unit{m}\) Use \(\log_{10}\), not \(\ln\): \(C_c\) is the slope per log cycle.
Void ratio after loading \(\Delta e=C_c\log\dfrac{\sigma'_{vf}}{\sigma'_{vo}}=\) \(0.3\log\dfrac{350.8}{150.8}\) \(=\) 0.110
\(e_f=e_0-\Delta e=\) \(1.161-0.110\) \(=\) 1.051 We should be able to calculate this void ratio and the new density.
