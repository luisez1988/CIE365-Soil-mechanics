Principles \(\delta_{pc}=\displaystyle\sum_i\delta_{pc,i}\)
(a) Single layer. Calculate effective stress at midpoint
\(\sigma'_{vo}=\underbrace{(\gamma_{sat}-\gamma_w)}_{\textcolor{red}{\gamma'}}\,\underbrace{z_i}_{\textcolor{red}{\text{mid depth}}}=\) \((125-62.4)\times12.5\) \(=\) 782.5 \(\unit{psf}\) The water table is at the surface, so the whole layer uses \(\gamma'\).
Calculate induced stress at midpoint Recall \(\Delta\sigma_v=I'q\)
Modeled as a strip load
\(\alpha=\tan^{-1}\left(\dfrac{B}{2z}\right)\)
\(I'=\underbrace{\dfrac{2}{\pi}}_{\textcolor{red}{\text{symmetry}}}\left[\alpha+\sin\alpha\cos\alpha\right]\)
\(\alpha=\) \(\tan^{-1}\left(\dfrac{15}{2\times12.5}\right)\) \(=\) \(31^{\circ}=0.540\) \(\unit{rad}\)
\(I'=\) \((2/\pi)[0.540+\sin31^{\circ}\cos31^{\circ}]\) \(=\) 0.625 \(\alpha\) alone must be in rad; two half-strips with \(\beta=0\) give the \(2/\pi\).
Induced stress
\(\Delta\sigma_v=I'q=\) \(0.625\times6000\) \(=\) 3749 \(\unit{psf}\) ksf to psf before multiplying.
Calculate final effective vert. stress
\(\sigma'_{vf}=\) \(782.5+3749\) \(=\) 4532 \(\unit{psf}\)
Check case \(\sigma'_c=OCR\cdot\sigma'_{vo}=\) \(1.5\times782.5\) \(=\) 1174 \(\unit{psf}\)
\(\sigma'_{vf}>\sigma'_c\) Compare \(\sigma'_{vf}\) with \(\sigma'_c\) to pick the case.
Calculate settlement
\(\delta_{pc}=\dfrac{H_0}{1+e_0}\left(C_r\log OCR+C_c\log\dfrac{\sigma'_{vf}}{\sigma'_c}\right)\)
\(\delta_{pc}=\) \(\dfrac{25}{1.95}\left(0.04\log1.5+0.33\log\dfrac{4532}{1174}\right)\)
\(\delta_{pc}=\) 2.57 \(\unit{ft}\)
(b) Repeat at discretized layers \(h=25/10=2.5\unit{ft}\) replaces \(H_0\) in each sub-layer Each row repeats the single-layer calculation at the midpoint of its sub-layer.
