## Statement

> Estimate the primary consolidation settlement for the clay in the profile below. The void ratio of the sand is 0.76 and the water content of the clay is 43%. The increase of vertical stress is estimated to be 200 kPa at the middle of the clay layer. Assume the soil above the water table to be saturated, $C_c=0.3$, $G_s=2.7$ for the clay, and $G_s=2.65$ for the sand.

The profile is in `FiguresGeneral/Profile_ex61.svg` (rendered and read): $3\ \mathrm{m}$ of fine clean sand above the water table, $6\ \mathrm{m}$ of the same sand below it, then $10\ \mathrm{m}$ of compressible clay over $5\ \mathrm{m}$ of coarse sand and gravel. The gravel does not enter the calculation.

## Given / Find

- Sand: $e = 0.76$, saturated throughout, $H_{sand} = 3 + 6 = 9\ \mathrm{m}$, water table at $3\ \mathrm{m}$ depth
- Clay: $w = 43\%$, $S = 100\%$, $G_s = 2.7$, $C_c = 0.3$, $H_0 = 10\ \mathrm{m}$
- $\Delta\sigma_v = 200\ \mathrm{kPa}$ at mid-depth of the clay, $\gamma_w = 9.81\ \mathrm{kN/m^3}$
- **Find:** $\delta_{pc}$

## Assumptions

The sand $G_s = 2.65$ was missing from the original statement. It is now part of the statement on the slide (see Review decisions).

The statement does not say whether the clay is normally consolidated. The example sits directly after the slide on normally consolidated settlement, no $OCR$ or $\sigma'_c$ is given, and only $C_c$ is supplied, so the clay is taken as normally consolidated ($\sigma'_c = \sigma'_{vo}$) and the stress path runs along the virgin curve.

The sand above the water table is saturated, as the statement says, so $\gamma_{sat}$ is used for the full $9\ \mathrm{m}$ of sand. Pore pressure is hydrostatic from the water table.

The clay is treated as a single layer, with $\sigma'_{vo}$ and $\Delta\sigma_v$ evaluated at its mid-depth, $z = 9 + 5 = 14\ \mathrm{m}$.

## Solution

The clay is normally consolidated, so loading moves it down the virgin compression line and the settlement is $\delta_{pc} = C_c \dfrac{H_0}{1+e_0}\log\dfrac{\sigma'_{vf}}{\sigma'_{vo}}$. Everything except $e_0$, $\sigma'_{vo}$ and $\sigma'_{vf}$ is given, so the work is finding those three.

1. Unit weight of the sand — $\gamma_{sat} = \dfrac{\gamma_w (G_s + e)}{1+e} = \dfrac{9.81 (2.65 + 0.76)}{1.76} = 19.01\ \mathrm{kN/m^3}$
2. Initial void ratio of the clay — $e_0 = w G_s = 0.43 \times 2.7 = 1.161$
3. Unit weight of the clay — $\gamma_{sat} = \dfrac{\gamma_w (G_s + e_0)}{1+e_0} = \dfrac{9.81 (2.7 + 1.161)}{2.161} = 17.53\ \mathrm{kN/m^3}$
4. Initial effective stress at mid-clay — $\sigma'_{vo} = \sigma_v - u = \underbrace{9 \times 19.01}_{\text{sand}} + \underbrace{5 \times 17.53}_{\text{clay}} - \underbrace{9.81 \times 11}_{u} = 171.09 + 87.65 - 107.91 = 150.8\ \mathrm{kPa}$
5. Final effective stress — $\sigma'_{vf} = \sigma'_{vo} + \Delta\sigma_v = 150.8 + 200 = 350.8\ \mathrm{kPa}$
6. Change in void ratio — $\Delta e = C_c \log\dfrac{\sigma'_{vf}}{\sigma'_{vo}} = 0.3 \log\dfrac{350.8}{150.8} = 0.3 \times 0.3667 = 0.110$, so $e_f = 1.161 - 0.110 = 1.051$
7. Settlement — $\delta_{pc} = C_c \dfrac{H_0}{1+e_0}\log\dfrac{\sigma'_{vf}}{\sigma'_{vo}} = \dfrac{0.3 \times 10}{2.161}\log\dfrac{350.8}{150.8} = \mathbf{0.509\ \mathrm{m}}$

Step 6 follows the right-hand panel of the handwritten solution, which draws the path on the $e$–$\log\sigma'_v$ plot. It also gives a check on step 7: $\delta_{pc} = \dfrac{H_0\,\Delta e}{1+e_0} = \dfrac{10 \times 0.110}{2.161} = 0.509\ \mathrm{m}$.
