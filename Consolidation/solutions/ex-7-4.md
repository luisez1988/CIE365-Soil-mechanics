## Statement

> Calculate the settlement due to primary consolidation for the soil profile shown in the figure below. Use: (a) a single layer assumption, and (b) 10 sub-layers.

This is a figure-dependent example. The data was read from `FiguresGeneral/complex_problem.svg` (rendered to PNG): a $25\ \mathrm{ft}$ layer of CH clay with the water table at the ground surface, $\gamma_{sat} = 125\ \mathrm{pcf}$, $e_0 = 0.95$, $OCR = 1.5$, $C_c = 0.33$, $C_r = 0.04$, resting on bedrock, loaded at the surface by a uniform load $q = 6\ \mathrm{ksf}$ over a width of $15\ \mathrm{ft}$. The figure originally read "$q = 6$ kips"; the label was corrected to ksf in the SVG. The strip-load influence factor comes from the `Induced_stress` deck (slide "Strip load", $I' = \frac{1}{\pi}[\alpha + \sin\alpha\cos(\alpha + 2\beta)]$).

## Given / Find

- Clay: $H_0 = 25\ \mathrm{ft}$, $\gamma_{sat} = 125\ \mathrm{pcf}$, $e_0 = 0.95$, $OCR = 1.5$, $C_c = 0.33$, $C_r = 0.04$
- Water table at the ground surface, $\gamma_w = 62.4\ \mathrm{pcf}$
- Surface load: $q = 6\ \mathrm{ksf} = 6000\ \mathrm{psf}$ over $B = 15\ \mathrm{ft}$
- **Find:** $\delta_{pc}$ under the centre of the load, (a) with one layer and (b) with 10 sub-layers

## Assumptions

None for the load: the figure now reads $q = 6\ \mathrm{ksf}$, which is what the handwritten solution uses.

The load is drawn in section only, with no length given, so it is modelled as a strip (infinitely long) load, as in the handwritten solution.

Settlement is computed under the centre line of the strip. By symmetry the strip is split into two halves of width $B/2$, each with $\beta = 0$ measured from the centre line, so $I' = \frac{2}{\pi}[\alpha + \sin\alpha\cos\alpha]$ with $\alpha = \tan^{-1}\!\left(\frac{B}{2z}\right)$.

The $OCR = 1.5$ applies throughout the layer, so $\sigma'_c = 1.5\,\sigma'_{vo}$ at every depth. The same $e_0$, $C_c$ and $C_r$ are used for every sub-layer.

The water table is at the surface, so the whole layer is submerged and $\sigma'_{vo} = (\gamma_{sat} - \gamma_w)\,z = \gamma' z$.

## Solution

The load spreads with depth, so $\Delta\sigma_v$ is largest near the surface and falls off below. A single layer uses the stresses at mid-depth only; splitting into sub-layers captures that variation. For each layer the settlement is the case-a or case-b formula from the previous examples, and the total is $\delta_{pc} = \sum_i \delta_{pc,i}$.

**(a) Single layer, evaluated at $z = 12.5\ \mathrm{ft}$**

1. Initial effective stress — $\sigma'_{vo} = (\gamma_{sat} - \gamma_w)\,z = (125 - 62.4) \times 12.5 = 782.5\ \mathrm{psf}$
2. Influence factor — $\alpha = \tan^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{B}{2z}\right) = \tan^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{15}{2 \times 12.5}\right) = 30.96^\circ = 0.540\ \mathrm{rad}$, and $I' = \dfrac{2}{\pi}[\alpha + \sin\alpha\cos\alpha] = \dfrac{2}{\pi}[0.540 + \sin 30.96^\circ \cos 30.96^\circ] = 0.625$
3. Induced stress — $\Delta\sigma_v = I' q = 0.625 \times 6000 = 3749\ \mathrm{psf}$
4. Final effective stress — $\sigma'_{vf} = \sigma'_{vo} + \Delta\sigma_v = 782.5 + 3749 = 4532\ \mathrm{psf}$
5. Check the case — $\sigma'_c = OCR \cdot \sigma'_{vo} = 1.5 \times 782.5 = 1174\ \mathrm{psf}$, so $\sigma'_{vf} > \sigma'_c$: case b
6. Settlement — $\delta_{pc} = \dfrac{H_0}{1+e_0}\left(C_r \log OCR + C_c \log\dfrac{\sigma'_{vf}}{\sigma'_c}\right) = \dfrac{25}{1.95}\left(0.04 \log 1.5 + 0.33 \log\dfrac{4532}{1174}\right) = \mathbf{2.57\ \mathrm{ft}}$

**(b) Ten sub-layers, $h = 2.5\ \mathrm{ft}$ each**

7. Repeat steps 1 to 6 at the centre of each sub-layer, $z_i = 1.25, 3.75, \dots, 23.75\ \mathrm{ft}$, with $H_0$ replaced by $h = 2.5\ \mathrm{ft}$. Every sub-layer is case b.

| $i$ | $z_i$ (ft) | $\sigma'_{vo}$ (psf) | $\sigma'_c$ (psf) | $\alpha$ (rad) | $I'$ | $\Delta\sigma_v$ (psf) | $\sigma'_{vf}$ (psf) | $\delta_{pc,i}$ (ft) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1.25 | 78.3 | 117.4 | 1.406 | 0.998 | 5989 | 6067 | 0.734 |

| 4 | 8.75 | 547.8 | 821.6 | 0.709 | 0.766 | 4594 | 5142 | 0.346 |
| 5 | 11.25 | 704.3 | 1056.4 | 0.588 | 0.668 | 4009 | 4713 | 0.284 |
| 6 | 13.75 | 860.8 | 1291.1 | 0.499 | 0.586 | 3513 | 4374 | 0.233 |

8. Total settlement — $\delta_{pc} = \sum_{i=1}^{10} \delta_{pc,i} = \mathbf{3.13\ \mathrm{ft}}$

The single-layer result is about 18% lower than the 10-sub-layer result. Because settlement depends on $\log\sigma'$, the shallow sub-layers, where $\sigma'_{vo}$ is small and $\Delta\sigma_v$ is large, contribute far more than the mid-depth values suggest.
