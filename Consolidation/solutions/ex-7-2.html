Data Sand: \(\gamma_{sat}=19.01\unit{kN/m^3}\)
Clay: \(G_s=2.7\), \(w=38\%\), \(OCR=4\), \(C_r=0.05\) The sand and the profile are unchanged from Example 7.1.
Principles \(\delta_{pc}=C_r\dfrac{H_0}{1+e_0}\log\dfrac{\sigma'_{vf}}{\sigma'_{vo}}\) Valid only while \(\sigma'_{vf}\le\sigma'_c\). Check it before using \(C_r\).
Find unit weight of clay
\(e_0=wG_s=\) \(0.38\times2.7\) \(=\) 1.026
\(\gamma_{sat}=\dfrac{\gamma_w(G_s+e_0)}{1+e_0}=\) \(\dfrac{9.81(2.7+1.026)}{2.026}\) \(=\) 18.04 \(\unit{kN/m^3}\) Only \(w\) changed, but that changes \(e_0\) and \(\gamma_{sat}\) of the clay.
Calculate effective stress at midpoint
\(\sigma'_{vo}=\) \(19.01\times9+18.04\times5-9.81\times11\) \(=\) 153.4 \(\unit{kPa}\)
Calculate final vert. effective stress
\(\sigma'_{vf}=\sigma'_{vo}+\Delta\sigma_v=\) \(153.4+200\) \(=\) 353.4 \(\unit{kPa}\)
Check case
\(\sigma'_c=OCR\cdot\sigma'_{vo}=\) \(4\times153.4\) \(=\) 613.4 \(\unit{kPa}\) \(>\sigma'_{vf}\) The stress path never reaches \(\sigma'_c\): it stays on the recompression line.
Calculate settlement
\(\delta_{pc}=\) \(\dfrac{0.05\times10}{2.026}\log\dfrac{353.4}{153.4}\) \(=\) 0.0895 \(\unit{m}\) \(C_r\) is much smaller than \(C_c\), so expect a much smaller settlement than 7.1.
