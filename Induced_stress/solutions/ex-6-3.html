Data \(B=L=2.5\unit{m}\), \(z=2\unit{m}\), \(q=16\unit{kPa}\)
Principles
\(I'=\dfrac{1}{2\pi}\left[\tan^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{LB}{zR_3}\right)+\dfrac{LBz}{R_3}\left(\dfrac{1}{R_1^2}+\dfrac{1}{R_2^2}\right)\right]\) This is the stress under a corner. Split the footing so the point is a corner of every piece.
First for point A \(L'=\) 1.25 \(\unit{m}\), \(B'=\) 1.25 \(\unit{m}\)
Distances
\(R_1=\) \(\sqrt{1.25^2+2^2}\) \(=\) 2.36 \(\unit{m}\)
\(R_2=\) 2.36 \(\unit{m}\)
\(R_3=\) \(\sqrt{2(1.25)^2+2^2}\) \(=\) 2.67 \(\unit{m}\)
Terms of \(I'_A\)
\(\tan^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{L'B'}{zR_3}\right)=\) \(\tan^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{1.25\times1.25}{2\times2.67}\right)\) \(=\) 0.2847 Express in radians
\(\dfrac{L'B'z}{R_3}=\) \(\dfrac{1.25\times1.25\times2}{2.67}\) \(=\) 1.1707 \(\unit{m}^{2}\)
\(\dfrac{1}{R_1^2}+\dfrac{1}{R_2^2}=\) \(\dfrac{1}{2.36^2}+\dfrac{1}{2.36^2}\) \(=\) 0.3596 \(1/\unit{m}^{2}\)
Influence factor \(I'_A=\) \(0.159\times[0.2847+1.1707\times0.3596]\) \(=\) 0.1123
\(\Delta\sigma_{zA}=\underbrace{4}_{\textcolor{red}{\substack{\text{because of}\\\text{symmetry}}}}q\,I'_A=\) \(4\times0.1123\times16\) \(=\) 7.19 \(\unit{kPa}\) Exact solution
For point B \(L=\) 2.5 \(\unit{m}\), \(B'=\) 1.25 \(\unit{m}\)
Distances
\(R_1=\) \(\sqrt{2.5^2+2^2}\) \(=\) 3.20 \(\unit{m}\)
\(R_2=\) \(\sqrt{1.25^2+2^2}\) \(=\) 2.36 \(\unit{m}\)
\(R_3=\) \(\sqrt{1.25^2+2.5^2+2^2}\) \(=\) 3.44 \(\unit{m}\) Not square now: \(R_1\ne R_2\)
Terms of \(I'_B\)
\(\tan^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{LB'}{zR_3}\right)=\) \(\tan^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{2.5\times1.25}{2\times3.44}\right)\) \(=\) 0.4267
\(\dfrac{LB'z}{R_3}=\) 1.8185 \(\unit{m}^{2}\),   \(\dfrac{1}{R_1^2}+\dfrac{1}{R_2^2}=\) 0.2773 \(1/\unit{m}^{2}\)
Influence factor \(I'_B=\) \(0.159\times[0.4267+1.8185\times0.2773]\) \(=\) 0.1482
\(\Delta\sigma_{zB}=2\,q\,I'_B=\) \(2\times0.1482\times16\) \(=\) 4.74 \(\unit{kPa}\)
