## Statement

> Solve Example 6.2 using the analytical and chart solutions for a rectangular loaded area. Compare with the approximation.

Revised on the author's request: "problem 6.2" now reads "Example 6.2".

Cross-referenced to Example 6.2 in the same deck: a square footing with $B = 2.5\ \mathrm{m}$ carrying $Q = 100\ \mathrm{kN}$, with the stress needed at $z = 2\ \mathrm{m}$ below the centre (A) and below the midpoint of an edge (B). The chart is the Newmark-type corner chart on the slide before Example 6.3, with $m = B/z$ and $n = L/z$.

## Given / Find

- From Example 6.2: $B = L = 2.5\ \mathrm{m}$, $q = Q/B^2 = 16\ \mathrm{kPa}$, $z = 2\ \mathrm{m}$
- **Find:** $\Delta\sigma_z$ at A and B using the corner formula and the chart, and compare with the $2 \times 2$ approximation of Example 6.2

## Assumptions

The footing is flexible and applies a uniform pressure $q = 16\ \mathrm{kPa}$, as in Example 6.2.

The corner formula gives the stress below a corner only. Points A and B are not corners, so the footing is split into rectangles that each have a corner at the point of interest, and the results are added by superposition.

The $\tan^{-1}$ term is evaluated in radians.

## Solution

The corner solution integrates Boussinesq's equation over a uniformly loaded rectangle: $\Delta\sigma_z = q\,I'$ with $I' = \dfrac{1}{2\pi}\left[\tan^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{LB}{zR_3}\right) + \dfrac{LBz}{R_3}\left(\dfrac{1}{R_1^2} + \dfrac{1}{R_2^2}\right)\right]$, $R_1 = \sqrt{L^2 + z^2}$, $R_2 = \sqrt{B^2 + z^2}$, $R_3 = \sqrt{L^2 + B^2 + z^2}$. Here $I'$ is dimensionless.

1. Split for A — four equal squares, each $L' = B' = 1.25\ \mathrm{m}$, with a shared corner at A
2. Distances for A — $R_1 = R_2 = \sqrt{1.25^2 + 2^2} = 2.36\ \mathrm{m}$, $R_3 = \sqrt{1.25^2 + 1.25^2 + 2^2} = 2.67\ \mathrm{m}$
3. Influence factor for A — $I'_A = \dfrac{1}{2\pi}\left[\tan^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{1.25 \times 1.25}{2 \times 2.67}\right) + \dfrac{1.25 \times 1.25 \times 2}{2.67}\left(\dfrac{1}{2.36^2} + \dfrac{1}{2.36^2}\right)\right] = \dfrac{1}{2\pi}\left[0.285 + 1.171 \times 0.360\right] = 0.112$
4. Stress at A — $\Delta\sigma_{zA} = 4\, q\, I'_A = 4 \times 16 \times 0.112 = \mathbf{7.19\ \mathrm{kPa}}$
5. Split for B — two equal rectangles, each $L = 2.5\ \mathrm{m}$ by $B' = 1.25\ \mathrm{m}$, with a shared corner at B
6. Distances for B — $R_1 = \sqrt{2.5^2 + 2^2} = 3.20\ \mathrm{m}$, $R_2 = \sqrt{1.25^2 + 2^2} = 2.36\ \mathrm{m}$, $R_3 = \sqrt{2.5^2 + 1.25^2 + 2^2} = 3.44\ \mathrm{m}$
7. Influence factor for B — $I'_B = \dfrac{1}{2\pi}\left[\tan^{-1}\!\left(\dfrac{2.5 \times 1.25}{2 \times 3.44}\right) + \dfrac{2.5 \times 1.25 \times 2}{3.44}\left(\dfrac{1}{3.20^2} + \dfrac{1}{2.36^2}\right)\right] = \dfrac{1}{2\pi}\left[0.427 + 1.819 \times 0.277\right] = 0.148$
8. Stress at B — $\Delta\sigma_{zB} = 2\, q\, I'_B = 2 \times 16 \times 0.148 = \mathbf{4.74\ \mathrm{kPa}}$
9. Chart — for A, $m = n = 1.25/2 = 0.625$ gives $I' \approx 0.11$ and $\Delta\sigma_{zA} \approx 4 \times 16 \times 0.11 = 7.0\ \mathrm{kPa}$. For B, $m = 1.25/2 = 0.625$ and $n = 2.5/2 = 1.25$ give $I' \approx 0.15$ and $\Delta\sigma_{zB} \approx 2 \times 16 \times 0.15 = 4.8\ \mathrm{kPa}$
10. Comparison — the $2 \times 2$ approximation of Example 6.2 gave 7.64 kPa at A and 4.91 kPa at B. These are $7.64/7.19 = 1.06$ and $4.91/4.74 = 1.04$ times the exact values, so the approximation errs on the high side by about 6% and 4%. The single point load gave 11.9 kPa at A, 66% too high.
