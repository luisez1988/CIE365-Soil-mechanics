Data \(z=2\unit{m}\),   \(Q=100\unit{kN}\),   \(B=2.5\unit{m}\)
Principles
\(\Delta\sigma_z=\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{n} q_i\,A_i\,I_i\),   \(I=\dfrac{3}{2\pi z^2\left[1+(r/z)^2\right]^{5/2}}\) Here \(I\) has units of \(1/\mathrm{m^2}\), so \(Q\,I\) is a stress.
Point load at A \(r=\) 0 \(\unit{m}\), \(z=2\unit{m}\)
\(I_A=\) \(\dfrac{3}{2\pi\,2^2\left[1+(0/2)^2\right]^{5/2}}\) \(=\) 0.119 \(\unit{/m^2}\)
\(\Delta\sigma_{zA}=Q\,I_A=\) \(100\times0.119\) \(=\) 11.9 \(\unit{kPa}\)
Point load at B \(r=\) \(2.5/2\) \(\unit{m}\), \(z=2\unit{m}\)
\(I_B=\) \(\dfrac{3}{2\pi\,2^2\left[1+(1.25/2)^2\right]^{5/2}}\) \(=\) 0.0523 \(\unit{/m^2}\)
\(\Delta\sigma_{zB}=Q\,I_B=\) \(100\times0.0523\) \(=\) 5.23 \(\unit{kPa}\)
Approximation with 4 sub-elements \(q=Q/B^2=\) \(100/2.5^2\) \(=\) 16 \(\unit{kPa}\) Uniform stress applied by footing
From figure \(r_1=\) \(\sqrt{(2.5/4)^2+(2.5/4)^2}\) \(=\) 0.884 \(\unit{m}\)
Near block \(I_1=\) \(\dfrac{3}{2\pi\,2^2\left[1+(0.884/2)^2\right]^{5/2}}\) \(=\) 0.0764 \(\unit{/m^2}\)
Block area \(A_1=\) \((2.5/2)^2\) \(=\) 1.5625 \(\unit{m^2}\)
Centerline (A) \(\Delta\sigma_{zA}=\underbrace{4}_{\textcolor{red}{\text{because of symmetry}}}q\,A_1\,I_1=\) \(4\times1.5625\times16\times0.0764\) \(=\) 7.64 \(\unit{kPa}\)
Mid edge (B), far blocks
\(r_2=\) \(\sqrt{(2.5/4)^2+(3\times2.5/4)^2}\) \(=\) 1.98 \(\unit{m}\)
\(I_2=\) \(\dfrac{3}{2\pi\,2^2\left[1+(1.98/2)^2\right]^{5/2}}\) \(=\) 0.0216 \(\unit{/m^2}\) From B, two blocks are near (\(r_1\)) and two are far (\(r_2\)).
Mid edge (B) \(\Delta\sigma_{zB}=\underbrace{2\,q\,A_1 I_1}_{\textcolor{red}{\text{near blocks}}}+\underbrace{2\,q\,A_1 I_2}_{\textcolor{red}{\text{far blocks}}}=\) \(3.82+1.08\) \(=\) 4.90 \(\unit{kPa}\)
Compare with the point load A: \(11.9/7.64=\) 1.56,   B: \(5.23/4.90=\) 1.07 The point load overestimates the stress, most of all right below it.
