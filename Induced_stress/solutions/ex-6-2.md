## Statement

> A square footing of 2.5 m of width is placed at the surface of the soil as shown in the figure. Structural analysis indicates that the footing will carry a load of 100 kN. Calculate the increment of stress at a depth of 2 m at the centerline and the mid edge of the footing. Use a discretization of two elements in each direction. How does the solution compare with the point load solution?

Revised on the author's request: "in in the figure" and "How does solution compares" are fixed.

The data is in the text. The figure (`FiguresGeneral/footing.svg`) gives the plan view. Point A is the centre of the footing and point B is the midpoint of one edge.

## Given / Find

- $B = L = 2.5\ \mathrm{m}$, $Q = 100\ \mathrm{kN}$, $z = 2\ \mathrm{m}$
- A: centre of the footing. B: midpoint of an edge.
- **Find:** $\Delta\sigma_z$ at A and B with a $2 \times 2$ discretization, and compare with a single point load $Q$

## Assumptions

The footing is flexible, so the 100 kN load is spread uniformly over the footing area as $q = Q/B^2$. The handwritten solution makes the same assumption.

The footing self-weight is neglected and the footing sits on the surface, so $z$ is measured from the base of the footing.

In the discretized solution the load on each sub-element, $q A_i$, acts as a point load at the sub-element's centroid.

The point load comparison places the whole 100 kN at the centre of the footing. For point B this gives $r = B/2$.

## Solution

Boussinesq's equation gives the stress below a point load in an elastic half-space. Written as $\Delta\sigma_z = Q\,I$, the influence factor is $I = \dfrac{3}{2\pi z^2\left[1 + (r/z)^2\right]^{5/2}}$ with units of $1/\mathrm{m^2}$. Superposition lets us add the effect of each sub-element: $\Delta\sigma_z = \sum q_i A_i I_i$.

1. Point load at A — $r = 0$: $I_A = \dfrac{3}{2\pi (2)^2 \left[1 + 0\right]^{5/2}} = 0.119\ \mathrm{m^{-2}}$, so $\Delta\sigma_{zA} = Q I_A = 100 \times 0.119 = 11.9\ \mathrm{kPa}$
2. Point load at B — $r = B/2 = 1.25\ \mathrm{m}$: $I_B = \dfrac{3}{2\pi (2)^2 \left[1 + (1.25/2)^2\right]^{5/2}} = 0.0523\ \mathrm{m^{-2}}$, so $\Delta\sigma_{zB} = 100 \times 0.0523 = 5.23\ \mathrm{kPa}$
3. Footing pressure and sub-element area — $q = \dfrac{Q}{B^2} = \dfrac{100}{2.5^2} = 16\ \mathrm{kPa}$, $A_i = \left(\dfrac{B}{2}\right)^2 = 1.25^2 = 1.5625\ \mathrm{m^2}$, so each sub-element carries $q A_i = 25\ \mathrm{kN}$
4. Centroid distances — each centroid is $B/4 = 0.625\ \mathrm{m}$ from the footing axes. From A all four centroids are at $r_1 = \sqrt{0.625^2 + 0.625^2} = 0.884\ \mathrm{m}$. From B the two near centroids are at $r_1 = 0.884\ \mathrm{m}$ and the two far ones at $r_2 = \sqrt{1.875^2 + 0.625^2} = 1.98\ \mathrm{m}$
5. Influence factors — $I_1 = \dfrac{3}{2\pi (2)^2 \left[1 + (0.884/2)^2\right]^{5/2}} = 0.0764\ \mathrm{m^{-2}}$ and $I_2 = \dfrac{3}{2\pi (2)^2 \left[1 + (1.98/2)^2\right]^{5/2}} = 0.0217\ \mathrm{m^{-2}}$
6. Centre, A — $\Delta\sigma_{zA} = 4\, q A_i I_1 = 4 \times 25 \times 0.0764 = \mathbf{7.64\ \mathrm{kPa}}$
7. Mid edge, B — $\Delta\sigma_{zB} = 2\, q A_i I_1 + 2\, q A_i I_2 = 2 \times 25 \times 0.0764 + 2 \times 25 \times 0.0217 = 3.82 + 1.09 = \mathbf{4.91\ \mathrm{kPa}}$
8. Comparison — the point load overestimates the stress by $11.9/7.64 = 1.56$ at A and $5.23/4.91 = 1.07$ at B. Concentrating the load at one point overstates the stress directly beneath it, and the error is smaller further from the load.

## Scaffolding decisions

Step 4 is the decision step. It needs a small plan view showing the four centroids, A and B. The $\sqrt{\ }$ expressions are blue blanks. Red note: from B, two centroids are near and two are far, so the four terms are not equal.

Step 3: $q = 16\ \mathrm{kPa}$ is blue. Red note: $q$ is the uniform pressure of a flexible footing, and $q A_i$ is the load each sub-element carries.

Steps 6 and 7 are green. Red note on step 6: by symmetry all four terms are the same. The handwritten solution says this too.

Step 8 is prose with the two ratios as blue blanks. It answers the last question in the statement, so it should not be left out.

Red note on step 1: the units of $I$ are $1/\mathrm{m^2}$ here, unlike the dimensionless $I'$ used later in the deck. Students mix these up in Example 6.3.

The influence factor substitutions in step 5 are long. On the slide I would show the bracket $\left[1 + (r/z)^2\right]^{5/2}$ in black and blank only $r$ and the result.
