Data \(\sigma_y=300\unit{kPa}\), \(\sigma_x=200\unit{kPa}\), \(\tau_{xy}=-75\unit{kPa}\), \(\alpha=25^\circ\) \(\tau_{xy}\) is clockwise
Principles many ways to solve. Method 1, using the full analytical solutions:
\(\sigma_\alpha=\dfrac{\sigma_x+\sigma_y}{2}+\dfrac{\sigma_y-\sigma_x}{2}\cos(2\alpha)+\tau_{xy}\sin(2\alpha)\)
\(\tau_\alpha=\dfrac{\sigma_y-\sigma_x}{2}\sin(2\alpha)-\tau_{xy}\cos(2\alpha)\)
(a) Principal stresses \(\tau_\alpha=0\)
\(\tan(2\alpha)=\dfrac{2\tau_{xy}}{\sigma_y-\sigma_x}\;\Rightarrow\; \boxed{\alpha=\dfrac{1}{2}\arctan\left(\dfrac{2\tau_{xy}}{\sigma_y-\sigma_x}\right)}\) Answer the question: What \(\alpha\) makes \(\tau=0\)?

Orientation \(\alpha=\) \(0.5\arctan\left(\dfrac{2(-75)}{300-200}\right)\) \(=\) \(-28.2\)\(^\circ\) Be careful to preserve the sign convention

Now find the principal stress
\(\sigma_\alpha=\) \(\dfrac{200+300}{2}+\dfrac{300-200}{2}\cos(-2\times28.2)\ldots\)
   \(\ldots+(-75)\sin(-2\times28.2)\)

\(\sigma_\alpha=\) \(250+27.7+62.4\) \(=\) 340.1 \(\unit{kPa}\)
Orthogonal direction \(\beta=90^\circ+\alpha\)
\(\beta=\) \(90-28.2\) \(=\) 61.8\(^\circ\)
Stress on the orthogonal plane
\(\sigma_\beta=\) \(\dfrac{200+300}{2}+\dfrac{300-200}{2}\cos(2\times61.8)\ldots\)
   \(\ldots+(-75)\sin(2\times61.8)\)

\(\sigma_\beta=\) \(250-27.7-62.4\) \(=\) 160 \(\unit{kPa}\)

Therefore \(\sigma_1=\) 340.1 \(\unit{kPa}\)   \(\sigma_3=\) 160 \(\unit{kPa}\)
(b) Normal stress on the plane at 25° from the horizontal
\(\sigma_\alpha=\dfrac{\sigma_x+\sigma_y}{2}+\dfrac{\sigma_y-\sigma_x}{2}\cos(2\alpha)+\tau_{xy}\sin(2\alpha)\)
\(\sigma_\alpha=\) \(\dfrac{200+300}{2}+\dfrac{300-200}{2}\cos(2\times25)\ldots\)
   \(\ldots+(-75)\sin(2\times25)\)

\(\sigma_\alpha=\) \(250+32.1-57.5\) \(=\) 225 \(\unit{kPa}\)

(b) Shear stress on the plane
\(\tau_\alpha=\dfrac{\sigma_y-\sigma_x}{2}\sin(2\alpha)-\tau_{xy}\cos(2\alpha)\)
\(\tau_\alpha=\) \(\dfrac{300-200}{2}\sin(2\times25)-(-75)\cos(2\times25)\)
\(\tau_\alpha=\) \(38.3+48.2\) \(=\) 86.5 \(\unit{kPa}\)
Method 2, simplified equations. First find the mean stress
\(\sigma_m=0.5(\sigma_x+\sigma_y)=\) \(0.5(200+300)\) \(=\) 250 \(\unit{kPa}\)

Deviatoric stress
\(\sigma_d=\sqrt{(\sigma_x-\sigma_y)^2+4\tau_{xy}^2}\)
\(\sigma_d=\) \(\sqrt{(200-300)^2+4\times75^2}\) \(=\) 180.3 \(\unit{kPa}\)

(a) Principal stresses \(\sigma_{1,3}=\sigma_m\pm0.5\sigma_d\)
\(\sigma_1=\) \(250+0.5\times180.3\) \(=\) 340.1 \(\unit{kPa}\)

\(\sigma_3=\) \(250-0.5\times180.3\) \(=\) 160 \(\unit{kPa}\)
(b) Stress acting at 25° from the horizontal
\(\sigma_\alpha=\sigma_m+0.5\sigma_d\cos(2\alpha)\) In this equation the angle is measured from the principal stresses
Normal stress
\(\sigma_\alpha=\) \(250+0.5\times180.3\cos(2(25+28.2))\) \(=\) 225 \(\unit{kPa}\) \(25+28.2\) is the angle to rotate from the principal stress configuration
Shear stress
\(\tau_\alpha=0.5\sigma_d\sin(2\alpha)\)
\(\tau_\alpha=\) \(0.5\times180.3\sin(2(25+28.2))\) \(=\) 86.5 \(\unit{kPa}\)
