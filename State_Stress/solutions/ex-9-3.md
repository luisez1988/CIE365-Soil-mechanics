## Statement

> The figure below shows a soil profile with horizontal water table. For pont A:  (a) Plot the Mohr's circles for the total and effective stress conditions. (b) Find the shear and normal stresses acting on a plane inclined at 38 degrees from the horizontal. (c) What is the effect of pore pressure on the shear stresses?

## Given / Find

- From `Profile.svg`: soft clay, water table $10\ \mathrm{ft}$ below the surface, point A at $30\ \mathrm{ft}$, $\gamma_{sat} = 125\ \mathrm{pcf}$, $K_o = 0.65$; $\gamma_w = 62.4\ \mathrm{pcf}$
- **Find:** (a) the total and effective stress Mohr's circles at A; (b) $\sigma_\alpha$, $\tau_\alpha$ and $\sigma'_\alpha$ on a plane at $38^\circ$ from the horizontal; (c) the effect of pore pressure on shear stress

## Assumptions

The figure gives only $\gamma_{sat}$, and the handwritten solution uses $125\ \mathrm{pcf}$ for the full $30\ \mathrm{ft}$, including the $10\ \mathrm{ft}$ above the water table. I kept that, so the clay above the water table is treated as saturated. This is an assumption the slide does not say out loud.

The pore pressure at A is hydrostatic below the water table: $u = 20 \times 62.4\ \mathrm{psf}$.

No shear acts on the horizontal and vertical planes under geostatic conditions, so $\sigma_v$ and $\sigma_h$ are principal stresses. $\sigma_v > \sigma_h$, so $\sigma_1 = \sigma_v$ acts on the horizontal plane, and $\alpha$ measured from the horizontal is also measured from the major principal plane.

## Solution

Find the in-situ vertical and horizontal stresses at A. These are the principal stresses, so they place each circle on the $\sigma$ axis. Then read the $38^\circ$ plane off the circles with the pole, and check it with the equations.

**Slide 1 — in-situ stresses** (beside the profile)

1. Data — given in the figure. Principles — in-situ stresses, Mohr's circle
2. 1) Vertical total stress — $\sigma_v = 125 \times 30 = 3750\ \mathrm{psf} = \mathbf{3.75\ \mathrm{ksf}}$
3. Vertical effective stress — $\sigma'_v = 3750 - (20 \times 62.4) = 2502\ \mathrm{psf} = \mathbf{2.5\ \mathrm{ksf}}$
4. 2) Horizontal effective stress — $\sigma'_h = 2.5 \times 0.65 = \mathbf{1.63\ \mathrm{ksf}}$
5. Horizontal total stress — $\sigma_h = 1.63 + (20 \times 62.4/1000) = \mathbf{2.88\ \mathrm{ksf}}$; the total and effective element sketches with these four values are kept as a figure

**Slide 2 — (a) and (b) graphically**

6. (a) Total stress circle — through $2.88$ and $3.75\ \mathrm{ksf}$; effective stress circle — through $1.63$ and $2.5\ \mathrm{ksf}$ (the handwritten graphical solution, kept, including the compass construction that finds the centre)
7. (b) Poles — at $(2.88, 0)$ and $(1.63, 0)$; a line at $38^\circ$ from each pole meets the circles at $\mathbf{(3.42, 0.42)}$ and $\mathbf{(2.17, 0.42)}$ (author's red note: shear stresses are not affected by pore pressure)

**Slide 3 — (b) by the equations** (beside the participation question)

8. Using equations, for the total stress first — $\sigma_m = 0.5(\sigma_v + \sigma_h) = 0.5(3.75 + 2.88) = 3.32\ \mathrm{ksf}$ and $\sigma_d = \sigma_1 - \sigma_3 = 3.75 - 2.88 = 0.87\ \mathrm{ksf}$
9. Normal stress — $\sigma_\alpha = \sigma_m + 0.5\sigma_d\cos(2\alpha) = 3.32 + 0.5 \times 0.87\cos(2 \times 38) = \mathbf{3.42\ \mathrm{ksf}}$
10. Shear stress — $\tau_\alpha = 0.5\sigma_d\sin(2\alpha) = 0.5 \times 0.87\sin(2 \times 38) = \mathbf{0.42\ \mathrm{ksf}}$
11. For the effective stress — $\tau_\alpha = \mathbf{0.42\ \mathrm{ksf}}$ (author's red note: because shear is not affected by pore pressure), and $\sigma'_\alpha = \sigma_\alpha - u = 3.42 - (20 \times 62.4/1000) = \mathbf{2.17\ \mathrm{ksf}}$
12. (c) Effect of pore pressure — none on the shear stress; it shifts the circle along the $\sigma$ axis by $u$. The slide asks this through the participation question beside the solution, so it is not answered in the solution itself.
