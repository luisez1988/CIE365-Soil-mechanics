## Statement

> Solve problem 7.1 using the pole method.

"Problem 7.1" is the element of Example 9.1 (`example62.svg`), which the slide repeats as a small sketch: $300\ \mathrm{kPa}$, $200\ \mathrm{kPa}$, $75\ \mathrm{kPa}$, and the plane at $25^\circ$ from the horizontal.

## Given / Find

- $\sigma_y = 300\ \mathrm{kPa}$, $\sigma_x = 200\ \mathrm{kPa}$, $\tau_{xy} = -75\ \mathrm{kPa}$, plane at $\alpha = 25^\circ$ from the horizontal (from Example 9.1)
- **Find:** the pole; $\sigma_1$, $\sigma_3$ and the orientation of $\sigma_1$; $\sigma_\alpha$ and $\tau_\alpha$ on the $25^\circ$ plane

## Assumptions

The data and the sign convention are those of Example 9.1. The $(\sigma, \tau)$ points are the ones plotted in Example 9.1 part (c): $(200, -75)$ for the vertical face and $(300, 75)$ for the horizontal face.

## Solution

The pole is the point on Mohr's circle where a line drawn parallel to a physical plane, through the point that represents the stresses on that plane, meets the circle again. A line from the pole parallel to any other plane then cuts the circle at the stresses on that plane. The whole solution is graphical, and the handwritten figure is kept as it is.

1. Data — $\sigma_y = 300\ \mathrm{kPa}$, $\sigma_x = 200\ \mathrm{kPa}$, $\tau_{xy} = -75\ \mathrm{kPa}$
2. Plot the circle — points $(200, -75)$ and $(300, 75)$, centre $250$, radius $90.1\ \mathrm{kPa}$
3. Find the pole, as numbered on the slide — 1) draw a line parallel to the vertical face through point 1, $(200, -75)$; 2) draw a line parallel to the horizontal face through point 2, $(300, 75)$; 3) the pole is the intersection, at $(200, 75)$
4. Principal stresses — $\sigma_3 = \mathbf{160\ \mathrm{kPa}}$ and $\sigma_1 = \mathbf{340\ \mathrm{kPa}}$, where the circle crosses the axis; the line from the pole to $\sigma_1$ is at $\mathbf{-28.2^\circ}$, the orientation of the major principal plane
5. Stresses on the plane — a line from the pole at $25^\circ$ meets the circle at $\sigma_\alpha = \mathbf{225\ \mathrm{kPa}}$ and $\tau_\alpha = \mathbf{87\ \mathrm{kPa}}$

The line from the pole at $25^\circ$ meets the circle at $(224.7, 86.5)$, the same as the analytical answer in Example 9.1. The figure's $87\ \mathrm{kPa}$ is a graphical reading of $86.5$.
