Data given in figure   Principles: in-situ stresses, Mohr's circle
Vertical total stress \(\sigma_v=\) \(125\times30\) \(=\) 3750 \(\unit{psf}=\) 3.75 \(\unit{ksf}\)
Vertical effective stress \(\sigma'_v=\) \(3750-(20\times62.4)\) \(=\) 2502 \(\unit{psf}=\) 2.5 \(\unit{ksf}\)
Horizontal effective stress \(\sigma'_h=\) \(2.5\times0.65\) \(=\) 1.63 \(\unit{ksf}\)
Horizontal total stress \(\sigma_h=\) \(1.63+(20\times62.4/1000)\) \(=\) 2.88 \(\unit{ksf}\)
(a), (b) Total and effective stress circles, and the plane at 38° from the pole
Using equations, for the total stress first
\(\sigma_m=0.5(\sigma_v+\sigma_h)=\) \(0.5(3.75+2.88)\) \(=\) 3.32 \(\unit{ksf}\)
\(\sigma_d=\sigma_1-\sigma_3=\) \(3.75-2.88\) \(=\) 0.87 \(\unit{ksf}\)

Normal stress \(\sigma_\alpha=\sigma_m+0.5\sigma_d\cos(2\alpha)\)
\(\sigma_\alpha=\) \(3.32+0.5\times0.87\cos(2\times38)\) \(=\) 3.42 \(\unit{ksf}\)

Shear stress \(\tau_\alpha=0.5\sigma_d\sin(2\alpha)\)
\(\tau_\alpha=\) \(0.5\times0.87\sin(2\times38)\) \(=\) 0.42 \(\unit{ksf}\)

For the effective stress \(\tau_\alpha=\) 0.42 \(\unit{ksf}\) Because shear is not affected by pore pressure

Effective normal stress \(\sigma'_\alpha=\sigma_\alpha-u=\) \(3.42-(20\times62.4/1000)\) \(=\) 2.17 \(\unit{ksf}\)
