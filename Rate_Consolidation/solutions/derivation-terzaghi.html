By mass conservation
\(q_{in}-q_{out}=\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial t}\;\longrightarrow\; q-\Big(\) \(q+\dfrac{\partial q}{\partial z}dz\) \(\Big)=\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial t}\;\longrightarrow\;-\dfrac{\partial q}{\partial z}dz=\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial t}\)
Invoking Darcy's law \(q=-k\dfrac{\partial h}{\partial z}\cdot 1\)
\(-\dfrac{\partial q}{\partial z}dz=\) \(k\dfrac{\partial^2 h}{\partial z^2}dz\) \(=k\underbrace{\dfrac{\partial^2 (z+h_w)}{\partial z^2}}_{\textcolor{red}{\text{Use total head definition}}}dz=k\dfrac{\partial^2 h_w}{\partial z^2}dz=\) \(\dfrac{k}{\gamma_w}\dfrac{\partial^2 u}{\partial z^2}dz\)
Invoking stress-strain relationships
\(\varepsilon_v=\varepsilon_a=-\dfrac{\Delta V}{V}\;\Longrightarrow\;\) \(\partial V=-V\,\partial\varepsilon_a\)   Thus: \(\dfrac{\partial V}{\partial t}=\) \(-V\dfrac{\partial\varepsilon_a}{\partial t}\) \(=\) \(-dz\cdot 1\,m_v\dfrac{\partial\sigma'}{\partial t}\)
Now invoke effective stress principle
\(\dfrac{\partial(\sigma-u)}{\partial t}=\) \(-\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial t}\) \(\;\longrightarrow\;\dfrac{k}{\gamma_w}\dfrac{\partial^2 u}{\partial z^2}\textcolor{red}{\cancel{\textcolor{black}{dz}}}=\textcolor{red}{\cancel{\textcolor{black}{dz}}}\,m_v\dfrac{\partial u}{\partial t}\)
