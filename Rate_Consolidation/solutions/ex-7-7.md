## Statement

> If the coefficient of secondary consolidation of the clay layer in problem 6.1 is $C_\alpha=0.0351$, what would the secondary compression settlements be after 10 yrs of completing primary consolidation settlements?

"Problem 6.1" is Example 7.1 (`Consolidation/solutions/ex-7-1.md`). The end of primary consolidation comes from Example 7.6.

## Given / Find

- From Example 7.1: $H_0 = 10\ \mathrm{m}$, $e_0 = 1.161$
- From Example 7.6: $t_{95} = 1.88\ \mathrm{yr}$ for the Boston blue clay
- $C_\alpha = 0.0351$, and $10\ \mathrm{yr}$ after primary consolidation ends
- **Find:** $\delta_{sc}$

## Assumptions

The clay is the Boston blue clay of Example 7.6, $C_v = 15\ \mathrm{m^2/yr}$. The statement does not say which clay, and the handwritten solution chooses this one.

Primary consolidation is taken to end at $95\%$ consolidation, so $t_{EOP} = t_{95} = 1.88\ \mathrm{yr}$. The theory reaches $100\%$ only at infinite time, so a practical end has to be chosen.

$e_0 = 1.161$ is taken from the approved Example 7.1. The handwritten solution uses $1.15$; see Flags.

## Solution

Secondary compression starts at the end of primary consolidation and grows with the log of time, $\delta_{sc} = \dfrac{C_\alpha H_0}{1+e_0}\log\left(\dfrac{t_f}{t_{EOP}}\right)$. This is the handwritten Principles line. The times in the log are measured from the start of loading.

1. End of primary consolidation — $t_{EOP} = t_{95} = 1.88\ \mathrm{yr}$, from Example 7.6
2. Final time — $t_f = t_{EOP} + 10 = 1.88 + 10 = 11.88\ \mathrm{yr}$
3. Secondary compression settlement — $\delta_{sc} = \dfrac{C_\alpha H_0}{1+e_0}\log\left(\dfrac{t_f}{t_{EOP}}\right) = \dfrac{0.0351 \times 10}{2.161}\log\left(\dfrac{11.88}{1.88}\right) = \mathbf{0.130\ \mathrm{m}}$

Units: $C_\alpha$ and the log are dimensionless, so $\delta_{sc}$ carries the units of $H_0$. $0.130\ \mathrm{m}$ is about a quarter of the primary settlement of $0.509\ \mathrm{m}$, which is a reasonable size for ten years of creep in a soft clay. The handwritten $0.131\ \mathrm{m}$ comes from $1 + e_0 = 2.15$.
