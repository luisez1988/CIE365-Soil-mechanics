Data \(\delta_{pc}=0.509\unit{m}\), \(C_v=15\unit{m^2/yr}\), \(C_v=0.4\unit{m^2/yr}\), \(U=95\%\)
Principles
\(\delta(t)=\delta_{pc}\,U(t)/100\)   \(T_v=\dfrac{C_v t}{H_{dr}^2}\)
Assume \(C_v=15\unit{m^2/yr}\)
\(T_v=\) 1.129 From table for \(U=95\%\).
\(t=\dfrac{T_v H_{dr}^2}{C_v}=\) \(\dfrac{1.129\times\overbrace{(5)^2}^{\textcolor{red}{\substack{\text{Because of}\\\text{double}\\\text{drainage}}}}}{15}\) \(=\) 1.88 \(\unit{yrs}\)
Assume \(C_v=0.4\unit{m^2/yr}\)
\(t=\) \(\dfrac{1.129\times(5)^2}{0.4}\) \(=\) 70.6 \(\unit{yrs}\) Depending on load and material, time can go up to centuries.
Calculate settlement vs. time
Use a list of known times, e.g. \(t=0\) to \(75\unit{yrs}\).
Sample calculation for \(t=25\unit{yrs}\)
a) for \(C_v=15\unit{m^2/yr}\): \(T_v=\dfrac{C_v t}{H_{dr}^2}=\) \(\dfrac{15\times25}{25}\) \(=\) 15
\(U[\%]=\left[1-\dfrac{8}{\pi^2}\exp(-\pi^2T_v/4)\right]\times100\)
\(\phantom{U[\%]}=\) \([1-0.811\times\exp(-9.87\times15/4)]\times100\) \(=\) 100\(\%\)
\(\delta(25\unit{yr})=\) \(100\times0.509/100\) \(=\) 0.509 \(\unit{m}\)
b) for \(C_v=0.4\unit{m^2/yr}\):
\(T_v=\dfrac{C_v t}{H_{dr}^2}=\) \(\dfrac{0.4\times25}{25}\) \(=\) 0.4
From table: \(U[\%]=\) 69.8 \(\%\)
\(\delta(25\unit{yr})=\) \(69.8\times0.509/100\) \(=\) 0.355 \(\unit{m}\) You can repeat this process in a spreadsheet.
