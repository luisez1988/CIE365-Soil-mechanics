Data \(U=50\%,\ 90\%,\) and \(95\%\)
Principles Approximation to solution
\(U=50\%\)
Use: \(T_v=\dfrac{\pi}{4}\left(U/100\right)^2\)
\(T_v=\) \(0.25\times\pi\times(0.5)^2\) \(=\) 0.196
\(U=90\%\)
Use: \(T_v=1.781-0.933\log(100-U)\)
\(T_v=\) \(1.781-0.933\times\log(100-90)\) \(=\) 0.848
\(U=95\%\)
Use: \(T_v=1.781-0.933\log(100-U)\)
\(T_v=\) \(1.781-0.933\times\log(100-95)\) \(=\) 1.129
