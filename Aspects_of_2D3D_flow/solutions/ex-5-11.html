Find \(H_{max}\)
Principles \(FS_p=\dfrac{i_{crit}}{i_{max}}=\) 1,   \(i_{crit}=\) \(\dfrac{\gamma'}{\gamma_w}\),   \(i=\dfrac{\Delta h}{L}\) Use \(\gamma'\), the submerged unit weight, not \(\gamma_{sat}\).
Recall quick condition happens if \(\sigma'_{vA}=\) 0; \(\ \sigma'_{vA}=\sigma_{vA}-u_A\) Sand is more susceptible to liquefaction. If this happens, it can make the cut unstable
Total stress at \(A\) \(\sigma_{vA}=\) \((10-H)\,\gamma_{sat,clay}\) Only \(10-H\) is left above \(A\).
Pore pressure at \(A\) \(u_A=\) \(6\,\gamma_w\) \(u_A\) set by piezometer level
Solve \(\sigma'_{vA}=0\) \((10-H)\,\gamma_{sat,clay}-6\,\gamma_w=0\)
\(H=\) \(\dfrac{10\,\gamma_{sat,clay}-6\,\gamma_w}{\gamma_{sat,clay}}\)
\(H=\) \(\dfrac{10(19)-6(9.81)}{19}\) \(=\) 6.90 \(\unit{m}\)
Second approach \(i_{crit}=i_{max}\) because \(FS_p=\) 1
Total heads, datum at \(A\) \(h_A=\) 6 \(\unit{m}\),   \(h_B=\) \(10-H\) The cut is kept dry, so\(h_{w,B=0}\)
Maximum gradient \(i_{max}=\dfrac{h_A-h_B}{10-H}=\) \(\dfrac{6-10+H}{10-H}\) \(=\) \(\dfrac{H-4}{10-H}\)
Critical gradient \(i_{crit}=\dfrac{\gamma_{sat}-\gamma_w}{\gamma_w}=\) \(\dfrac{19-9.81}{9.81}\) \(=\) 0.937
Solve \(i_{max}=i_{crit}\) \(\tfrac{H-4}{10-H}=0.937\implies H-4=\) \(9.37-0.937H\)
\(1.937H=\) 13.37 \(\implies H=\) 6.90 \(\unit{m}\)
